# WHO Pediatric Z-Score Growth Evaluation App

A simple web app that calculates child growth Z-scores from age, sex and
measurements, to help health workers assess growth and nutritional status.

## Indicators
- Weight-for-age
- Height-for-age
- BMI-for-age

## Data source
Official WHO Child Growth Standards reference tables (Excel files
published by the World Health Organization).

## How to use
Open the app here: https://pgland.github.io/Zscore-2/

Enter the child's sex, age, weight and height. The app displays the Z-scores.
